About Titan Offshore

Trusted commercial marine services backed by deep industry expertise

Titan Offshore LLC is a privately-held Texas limited liability company focused on the acquisition, operation, and disposition of commercial marine assets and offshore oil and gas equipment. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, our operations extend across the Gulf of Mexico through partner relationships in Louisiana and Texas.

Our business model centers on identifying undervalued offshore assets, executing clean transactions, and either operating them productively or restoring them to commercial service for resale. We work directly with major industry counterparties including drilling contractors, vessel operators, and oilfield service providers.

Titan Offshore is registered with the U.S. Government System for Award Management (SAM.gov) and maintains an active CAGE Code (0AVQ6) for federal contracting eligibility.

Our Services

Comprehensive offshore marine and oilfield asset solutions

Vessel Acquisition

Strategic acquisition of commercial marine vessels including crew boats, supply vessels, and specialized offshore equipment from drilling contractors and operators.

Asset Operations

Day-to-day operational management of offshore assets including drilling rigs, barges, and support vessels in the U.S. Gulf of Mexico.

Vessel Chartering

Bareboat and time charter arrangements for U.S. Coast Guard certified passenger vessels supporting offshore operations.

Marine Equipment

Procurement and resale of marine diesel engines, propulsion equipment, generator sets, and offshore parts inventory.

Capital Advisory

Strategic capital structuring for offshore asset acquisitions, including financing introductions and transaction support.

Government Contracting
